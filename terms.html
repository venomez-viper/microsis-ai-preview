VoiceForge Acoustic Consent, Cloning & Provenance

MicroSIS VoiceForge enables studio-grade voice cloning from 30 seconds of reference audio and sub-40ms conversational speech synthesis. To prevent voice impersonation fraud, vishing, and deceptive media:

  • Mandatory Speaker Verification: Creating a custom voice clone requires cryptographic verification that the speaker has provided explicit, informed consent (via live challenge-phrase recording or executed talent release).
  • Imperceptible Cryptographic Watermarking: Every audio stream synthesized by VoiceForge embeds a tamper-evident, psychoacoustically imperceptible SHA-256 provenance watermark (Draupadi · 48kHz Neural PCM) allowing instant verification of synthetic origin.
  • Automated Agent Disclosure: Where VoiceForge conversational agents interact with consumers over outbound or inbound PSTN/SIP telephony in regulated jurisdictions, Customer must configure appropriate initial disclosure informing the caller that they are speaking with an AI assistant.
Policy 03

Human-in-the-Loop Requirements for Consequential Decisions

When deploying DocMind, ComplianceAI, or PredictFlow in high-consequence workflows (such as clinical prior-authorization triage, credit underwriting, insurance claims adjudication, legal discovery, or employment screening), Customer must maintain meaningful human oversight and review the coordinate bounding boxes, confidence intervals, and directional SHAP explanations provided by the Platform before taking final adverse action affecting an individual.
